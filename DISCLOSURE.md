# Public disclosure scope / 公开范围

This repository presents AIWG's research question, the author's role, a conceptual relationship between assets, activity groups and spaces, and three curated development stills. The English and Chinese overviews mirror the project website.

The nested figure describes scales, not model stages or generation order. Higher-level learning remains experimental. The scene images show visual development; they do not establish complete interaction reliability, an advantage over baselines or research effectiveness. The manuscript is unfinished and is not included.

## Kept private

Source code, Unreal projects, unpublished manuscripts and findings, training data and models, dataset inventories, experimental numbers, hyperparameters, prompts, schemas, operational rules, scoring instruments, evaluation protocols, participant records, logs, credentials and third-party source assets are excluded. The repository does not contain the development repository's history.

Only the author-approved overview, conceptual figure and selected scene images may be synchronized here. An update to a private project is not permission to publish it. Any additional material needs a separate disclosure review and author approval.

## Limits

Public concepts and images can be copied or imitated. This boundary reduces disclosure; it does not make public information confidential. Removing a file later cannot retract copies, forks, caches or public Git history.
